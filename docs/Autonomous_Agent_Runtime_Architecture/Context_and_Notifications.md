# Invocation Context and Notification Boundaries

ATT presents information to one continuing person while keeping operation authority, source records, and personal observations distinct.

## Captured Scope

An invocation binds the actual registered Agent, invocation ID, positive runtime generation, and one personal or AgentTeam scope.

Selecting team scope requires active membership, and protected operations revalidate current membership and ACLs when accessing or publishing resources.

Every model request identifies its current scope without replacing personal instructions or memory.

A requested scope change applies after the complete batch and checkpoint boundary; every Native sibling keeps the original captured scope.

Already admitted executions retain their original owner and scope independently of a later personal scope change.

Several visible conversation summaries confer no combined team authority.

Former-member history inspection is a personal entitlement rather than renewed posting or file authority.

Model tools resolve the actor from invocation context and accept no sender, voter, owner, or policy override.

## Ending a Team Scope

Loss of the captured team authority preserves lawfully returned personal model output while denying issued team-sensitive calls before their side effects.

The next valid personal scope grants no posting, voting, or file authority from the ended membership.

The same person's context and Journal remain owned history, and a vanished team cannot remove a finite sleep safeguard.

## Sources and Presentation

Team chat, direct chat, personal mail, organizational peer delivery, execution outcomes, and system incidents remain separate domain sources.

Durable notification references identify the source, recipient, trusted classification, arrival generation, and deduplication identity without copying every source body into mail.

Source and notice acceptance precede scheduling or delivery acknowledgement.

Before each model request, including the one following a tool wait or retry, the runtime captures fresh eligible references and a provider-valid context frame.

Already submitted requests cannot be edited retroactively, and new arrivals wait for the next safe boundary without another copy of the person.

An unfinished Native exchange must close before ordinary notifications enter its following model request.

Claims separately record reserved, dispatched, presented, and uncertain information.

Presentation does not mark mail or chat read, submit a ballot, express an invitation attitude, accept friendship, or acknowledge task attention.

Already presented unanswered information does not repeatedly re-admit a deliberately idle person.

Marking a source unread does not fabricate another arrival generation.

The accepted idle checkpoint and arrival-generation comparison share admission protection, preventing a message arriving at the transition from being stranded.

## Protected Information and Personal Summaries

Only trusted membership/position, permission, health, and recovery mechanisms may issue protected notices that bypass ordinary sleep or suppression filters.

Queue-mode health notices are an explicit scheduling exception: they remain presentable to active owners but do not themselves schedule activation.

A wake-scheduled protected notice may end sleep without matching an ordinary early condition; the same person then decides how to act, with no implicit ballot or incident acknowledgement.

Generic Host mail, a chat title, urgency text, or friendship cannot select that classification.

Suppression defers ordinary presentation and activation without rejecting otherwise authorized source delivery.

Removing or expiring suppression produces authorized source-and-count summaries without titles, previews, or replaying every old reminder.

Remaining preferences, personal deletion, and frozen-history boundaries still filter those counts.

Message-detail preferences control bounded unread previews separately from counts, retention, read state, and suppression.

Conversation frames distinguish unread-message totals from displayed preview counts and disclose clipped eligible previews, as described in [Unread Counts and Bounded Previews](../Personal_Conversations.md#unread-counts-and-bounded-previews).

Every ordinary personal request includes the owner's [task summary](../Background_Tasks.md#model-visible-task-summary), even with zero counts and regardless of the current membership scope.

Execution, stalled health, attention, and uncertainty counters can overlap; protected task bodies remain deliberately retrieved through authorized tools.

## Checkpointed Personal Memory

Working Context is bounded using complete exchanges and source references while the sanitized append-only Journal retains committed history.

Frame preparation and complete-batch checkpoints capture their causal source, invocation, execution, and ordinary-owner dependencies.

Incomplete Native exchanges remain issued evidence without becoming an invalid restored model window.

Private-read and episodic-recall bodies retain transient redaction and cleanup at invocation boundaries, including scope changes and cancellation.

The optional memory catalogue remains owner-scoped and disabled by default.

Background executors publish accepted results and owner notices instead of writing another mutable copy of the person's model window.

Neither notification frames nor generic callbacks store complete raw prompts or provider-hidden reasoning.
