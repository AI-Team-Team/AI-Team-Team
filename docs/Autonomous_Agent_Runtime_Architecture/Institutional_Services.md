# Institutional Authority on the Common Runtime

ATT's institutions own their rules and authoritative transitions while using the common runtime to execute the same continuing Agents.

Scheduling, notification presentation, ordinary answers, and technical checkpoints do not supply consent or a collective decision.

## Cases and Personal Ballots

A governance case captures its actual AgentTeam or Agent principal, business reference, strict schema, frozen eligible voters, exact membership intervals, and electorate revision.

Voters receive addressed personal mail explaining the explicit available choice tools and may choose whether to handle the matter.

Opening or reading the mail, publishing chat, or returning JSON in ordinary model output is not a ballot.

When complete participation is required, every frozen eligible voter must submit a valid immutable choice before a strict majority supplies authority.

Missing votes, invalid literal booleans or aliases, ties, or changed eligibility supply no authority.

A changed electorate creates another exact case without reusing votes for changed membership.

Already accepted ballots survive unrelated activity errors and observer cancellation.

Root votes only through its own explicit Agent identity; member order and creator provenance never select representatives.

## Communication

`ATTConfig.communication` chooses permissive, parent-approval, or lineage-approval institutions for AgentTeam endpoints.

Queued requests deliver voluntary personal ballots rather than wait for a team discussion or force an immediate choice.

Communication-approval `wake` is unsupported; protected notices and independently configured health escalation retain their separate semantics.

An independent endpoint is exempt only from its own parent/ancestor requirement, preserving applicable attached-counterpart authority.

Independent pairs communicate without a Request or Agreement, while cross-tree attached routes include real independent tops without inventing a common Root.

Requests bind immutable policy snapshots and relevant routes, stale routes receive successors, and active Agreements retain directional lifetime until endpoint revocation.

Accepted peer delivery is idempotent by invocation operation and requires its exact source receipt.

## Formation and Topology

Formation preserves exact-revision invitation consent and all four public attitudes independently of mail state.

Deliberate `None` withholds a public attitude and remains externally indistinguishable from an unanswered invitation.

Accepted subsets, minimum creation size, final initiator confirmation, abandonment, and explicit late joining retain their own institutional rules.

Optional candidate preparation provides validated advice without becoming a mandatory all-member deliberation or membership grant.

Creation publishes accepted identities, libraries, membership intervals, and initial addressed work without synchronously waiting for child business completion.

Ordinary later activity failure preserves an already accepted team.

Teams have an explicit Agent, AgentTeam, or absent organizational parent independent of membership and creator provenance.

Reattachment requires prospective-parent acceptance, while default-enabled Host-configurable unilateral detachment requires no old-parent approval.

Successful own-position transitions atomically validate and record the ten-minute cooldown; failed requests and inherited ancestor movement do not consume it.

Approval is followed by final original-parent, route, lifecycle, cycle, Host-policy, and cooldown revalidation at accepted publication.

Actual parent loss makes surviving direct children independent without flattening descendants or changing members' owned state.

## Resource Decisions and Health

Parent failover uses an independently bounded default 120-second resource attempt with explicit eligible choices.

Self-parent or shared-electorate conflicts fail closed without voter substitution or automatic-selection fallback.

Late choices cannot change a model after the exact attempt expires, and accepted selection rechecks binding, position, eligibility, deadline, and the atomic quota ledger.

Each health assessment creates a real temporary restricted team with fresh participants on the same scheduler and execution services.

All predefined auditors must supply valid structured findings before strict-majority aggregation; missing findings, failure, ties, or deadline expiry produce UNKNOWN.

Health conclusions do not authorize business repair, permission changes, membership changes, or model replacement.

Confirmed integrity faults receive immediate deterministic protection without waiting for an audit.

Incidents retain stable fingerprints, occurrence history, claims, and explicit acknowledgement without TTL or hard dropping limits.

Ending activity or presenting a notice does not acknowledge an incident.
