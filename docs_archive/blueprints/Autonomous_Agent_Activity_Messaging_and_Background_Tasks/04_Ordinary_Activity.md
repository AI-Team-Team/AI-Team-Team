# 4. Ordinary Activity Without Discussion Rounds

[Back to blueprint index](README.md).

## Confirmed Direction

An AI may choose to speak, read, continue working, wait, or remain silent without waiting for a numbered discussion round.

There is no all-member barrier requiring every team member to produce an answer before someone can act again.

There is no automatic publication of every model response as an utterance to the team.

Explicit messaging tools publish what the Agent deliberately chooses to say.

The framework must not appoint a leader, diplomat, or business-progress evaluator merely to make the conversation converge.

Message sequences, invocation IDs, timestamps, and execution attempts remain useful records without constituting new discussion rounds.

## Stopping Is Not Completion

An Agent may stop its current participation without obtaining a team vote.

Stopping speaking does not automatically leave the AgentTeam, retire the Agent, cancel its personal tasks, or declare the work complete.

Not speaking means only that the person has not published a message and does not prevent reading, tool use, writing artifacts, or other personal activity.

Silence and an empty event queue describe current activity, not a certified outcome.

Choosing not to speak must not itself be recorded as a failed member interaction or unhealthy behavior.

One person's decision to stop speaking must not close the AgentTeam's shared conversation space or prevent other members from continuing.

Ending one model invocation also does not establish that the person has finished everything it is doing.

## Completion Judgments

The AIs may vote on whether they believe a particular piece of work is complete and does not need further pursuit.

Such a vote records a personal or collective judgment rather than a universal framework certification of success.

No fixed electorate, majority threshold, unanimity requirement, requester-acceptance gate, or binding effect has been agreed for these judgments.

Reading a message, stopping activity, acknowledging a notification, and casting a completion vote must remain different operations.

How the AIs establish and record their own work-specific procedures remains to be designed.

For the framework's boundaries on work-specific procedures, refer to the [implementation plan](../Implementation_plan_Autonomous_Agent_Activity_Messaging_and_Background_Tasks/01_Scope.md).
