# 2. Five Different Concepts

[Back to blueprint index](README.md).

## Confirmed Direction

| Concept | Question it answers | Important distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | Which individual owns the identity and personal state? | A continuing person with personal identity, memory, and artifacts. |
| AgentTeam | Which organization supplies the current relationship and authority context? | An organization and relationship context, not an owner of its members' identities. |
| WorkItem | Which outcome or activity is being described? | A conceptual description of work that the AIs may maintain in DocLibs, not a required new framework object. |
| Discussion | Which subject is being exchanged or deliberated? | An exchange or topic, not a compulsory all-member round sequence. |
| BackgroundTask | Which execution is being or has been performed? | A tracked execution with factual state, not proof that the associated work is complete. |

An outcome may be open-ended, exploratory, creative, or revised by the participating AIs.

A WorkItem must not require a universally measurable result merely because the framework can store a record for it.

One team can undertake several pieces of work, and one piece of work can involve several conversations and background executions.

A personal activity need not become team-owned just because its owner also belongs to a team.

Not every conversation or message must be forced into a WorkItem.

The five concepts do not require five new runtime models or database tables.

WorkItem remains a conceptual distinction, with existing personal and team DocLibs available for AI-authored work records rather than a new compulsory work-management interface.
