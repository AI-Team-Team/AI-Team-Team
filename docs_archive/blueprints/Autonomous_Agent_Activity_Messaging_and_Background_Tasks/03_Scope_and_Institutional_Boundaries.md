# 3. Initial Scope and Institutional Boundaries

[Back to blueprint index](README.md).

## Confirmed Direction

The initial replacement of round-based discussion applies to ordinary business activity.

Creative writing, development, research, and other kinds of work do not need a common framework-defined acceptance procedure.

Any work-specific criteria, reviewing roles, coordination arrangements, or completion procedures should be established by the AIs themselves rather than assumed by ATT.

Formal governance decisions and health supervision have separate contracts that must be reviewed explicitly before their execution mechanisms change.

For the complete replacement scope, including governance and supervision, refer to the [implementation plan](../Implementation_plan_Autonomous_Agent_Activity_Messaging_and_Background_Tasks/01_Scope.md).

Free conversation does not weaken membership consent, governance authorization, communication policy, document ACLs, or strict state-integrity requirements.

Existing AgentTeam-to-AgentTeam communication Agreements remain distinct from an individual's membership conversation and personal friendships.
